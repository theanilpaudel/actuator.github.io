## 🛠️ Common Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Syncing & Cloud Data
* **Verify Network Connection:** Ensure you have an active Wi-Fi or cellular data connection.
* **Force Refresh:** Pull down on the main dashboard screen to force an instant reconciliation with the cloud server.
* **Account Status:** Go to **Settings > Account** and confirm that your session is active.

### 2. Notifications & Background Sync
* **iOS Users:** Open iOS **Settings > {{ site.title }} > Notifications** and ensure "Allow Notifications" and "Background App Refresh" are enabled.
* **Android Users:** Open Android **Settings > Apps > {{ site.title }} > Battery** and set battery usage to "Unrestricted" or "Optimized" (avoid "Restricted").

### 3. App Updates
Make sure you are running the latest version of the app. Updates include critical bug fixes, performance improvements, and security patches:
* [Download latest version from Apple App Store]({{ site.app_store_url }})
* [Download latest version from Google Play Store]({{ site.google_play_url }})

### 4. Account & Data Deletion
In compliance with Apple and Google data privacy requirements, you can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from within the app:
1. Open the {{ site.title }} app.
2. Tap **Settings > Account & Security > Delete Account**.
3. Confirm deletion. All cloud backups and profile details will be permanently removed within 30 days.

You can also submit an account deletion request by emailing [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}) with the subject line *"Account Deletion Request"*.
